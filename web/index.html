How it works

Hold, and you are hired

Holding shares is employment. Nothing to stake, nothing to lock, nothing to claim. Selling is the only way to resign.

Weight, not headcount

Wages divide by shares × seniority × attendance. Seniority runs 1× to 4× over three quarters and cannot be bought — only served.

The treasury cannot be drained

Each period pays a percentage of what remains, halving quarterly to a 0.50% floor. It converges; it never empties.

3% wages, 1% buyback

Revenue splits the moment it lands. The buyback buys $INC and retires it, and can never reach into wages already earned.

Paid in what it trades in

A company denominated in a stablecoin earns and pays in that stablecoin. Any currency the registry approves works.

Books you can audit

Every cheque is recorded against company, period and employee — which is what makes paying a period twice impossible rather than merely unlikely.
